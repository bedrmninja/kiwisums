How Kiwi households actually spend

Stats NZ's Household Economic Survey put average weekly household spending at around $1,598 in the year to June 2023 — call it roughly $1,550-$1,750 a week in 2026 after inflation and wage growth, or about $6,700-$7,600 a month. Housing is the single biggest slice for most households: 18-22% of spending for homeowners with a mortgage, and noticeably higher, often 28-35%, for renters. Food is usually the second-largest category, typically 15-18% of total spending.

These are national averages across singles, couples and families of every size, so treat them as a rough compass rather than a rulebook — a single person in a share house and a family of five will land in very different places.
