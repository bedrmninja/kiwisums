Comprehensive vs third party: what's the difference?

Comprehensive cover pays for damage to your own car regardless of fault, plus damage you cause to others. Third party fire & theft adds cover for your car being stolen or damaged by fire, on top of the basic third-party liability. Third party only covers damage you cause to someone else's property — nothing for your own car.

Why do young drivers pay more?

Statistically, younger and less experienced drivers are involved in more at-fault accidents, so insurers price that higher claims risk into the premium. The gap narrows with age and a clean driving record, and can often be reduced with a higher excess or a restricted-driver discount for some policies.
