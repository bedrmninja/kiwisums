Methodology

Afterpay: $17 per missed payment (an initial $10 fee plus $7 if still unpaid after about a week), capped at the lesser of 25% of the order or $68. Laybuy: $10 flat per missed payment, capped at $40.

Included

Late fees only, based on the number of missed payments you specify.

Not included

Any account suspension effects, impact on future BNPL approval, or interactions with your bank's own overdraft or dishonour fees if a payment fails.

Why BNPL feels free until it isn't

Buy Now Pay Later providers make their money mainly from merchant fees, not from customers, which is why there's no interest charge if you pay on time. The risk is entirely in missed payments — late fees are flat dollar amounts rather than percentage-based interest, which means they hit hardest on smaller purchases and add up fast if you're juggling several BNPL purchases at once and miss payments on more than one.

BNPL purchases also don't build any credit history the way responsible credit card use can, and multiple concurrent BNPL commitments can be easy to lose track of since each provider tracks your payments separately — worth keeping a simple list if you use more than one.
