What's in this estimate

This calculator adds together your fuel or electricity cost, road user charges (RUC) where applicable, registration (rego), warrant of fitness (WOF), insurance, and servicing/tyres to give a realistic annual and weekly running cost. Diesel and electric vehicles pay RUC — currently around $76 per 1,000km for light vehicles — instead of, or in addition to, fuel excise, which is why they're broken out separately.

It doesn't include depreciation, which for many vehicles is actually the single largest cost of ownership, or finance costs if you're paying the car off — use the car finance calculator alongside this one if that applies to you.
