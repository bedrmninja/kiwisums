Is private health insurance worth it in NZ?

NZ's public health system covers urgent and emergency care for everyone — the real question is what private cover buys you on top, and whether that's worth the monthly premium.

What public healthcare actually covers

New Zealand's public health system covers emergency and urgent care for everyone, regardless of insurance status — if you're seriously injured or acutely unwell, you'll be treated. Where the public system is more strained is non-urgent elective procedures (like hip replacements or cataract surgery), which can carry waitlists of weeks to many months depending on your region and the specific procedure.

What private health insurance adds

Private cover mainly buys you speed and choice: skipping the public waitlist for elective surgery, choosing your own specialist and hospital, and sometimes a private room. Comprehensive policies go further, adding specialist consultations and diagnostic scans outside hospital too — useful if you'd otherwise pay those costs out of pocket.

"Private insurance rarely buys better emergency care — it mostly buys you a shorter wait for the procedures that aren't urgent yet."

Public vs private, side by side

What matters Public system Private insurance
Emergency & urgent care Covered for everyone, no wait for genuine emergencies Adds little here — public emergency care is already free and fast
Non-urgent elective surgery Free, but can involve a long wait Usually the biggest single benefit — much faster access
Choice of specialist/hospital Limited — assigned within the public system You choose, within your policy's network
Monthly cost Funded through general taxation An ongoing premium, rising with age

Both systems have their place — many NZ households use public care as the default and add private cover specifically to skip elective surgery waitlists.

What matters more to you?

If avoiding a long wait for elective surgery and choosing your own specialist matters most, private cover — even a lower-cost surgical-only policy — is likely to feel worthwhile. Check the health insurance cost calculator for a premium estimate.
