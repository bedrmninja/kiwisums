How much does the average NZ household spend each month?

Wondering if your spending is normal? Here's how an average New Zealand household's monthly budget breaks down.

The average household budget breakdown

Housing — rent or mortgage repayments — is by far the biggest line in most NZ household budgets, commonly somewhere between 30-40% of take-home pay, and often more in Auckland or Wellington. Food, transport, utilities and insurance round out most of what's left.

After the big three (housing, food, transport), the remainder typically splits across utilities, insurance, healthcare, and discretionary spending like entertainment and eating out — the category that usually has the most room to flex.

Category Typical % of spend
Housing 30-40%
Food & groceries 15-20%
Transport 10-15%
Utilities 4-8%
Insurance 3-6%
Discretionary / entertainment 10-15%

Percentages are illustrative averages and will vary a lot by household size, region and life stage — use them as a rough benchmark, not a target.

"A budget isn't about restriction — it's about visibility. Most overspending happens in categories nobody's actually looking at."

Where most households overspend without realising

Subscriptions, food delivery, and small discretionary purchases are the classic culprits — individually tiny, collectively significant, and easy to lose track of because they don't show up as one big bill.
